
# Disaster Relief Connect (DRC)
<img width="500" height="500" alt="logo" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/032efb23-6b10-49e8-a3ad-af4987626c9d" />

Disaster Relief Connect (DRC) is a web-based coordination platform designed to improve communication and resource visibility among rescue agencies during disaster situations.

The project focuses on solving one core problem: **lack of coordination between independent rescue agencies when time and information matter the most**.

---

## 🚨 Problem Statement

During disasters such as floods, earthquakes, fires, or cyclones:

* Rescue agencies work independently with limited coordination
* Real-time information about nearby agencies is missing
* Resource availability is unclear
* Communication relies on manual or delayed channels

The result is inefficient response, duplicated effort, and delayed rescue operations.

---

## 💡 Solution

DRC provides a centralized system where rescue agencies can:

* Register themselves and their operational details
* Share their location using GPS-based tracking
* List available resources (vehicles, medical aid, personnel)
* Discover and coordinate with nearby agencies

The goal is **faster decision-making and better utilization of available resources**.

---

## ✨ Features

* **Agency Registration**

  * Name, type, and operational area
* **Agency Discovery**

  * Find nearby rescue agencies during emergencies
* **GPS-Based Location Tracking**

  * Enables proximity-based coordination
* **Resource Mapping**

  * Visibility into available rescue resources
* **Scalable Architecture**

  * Designed to support real-time features in future
